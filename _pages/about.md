I received my Ph.D. in Life Science from [Dongguk University](https://www.dongguk.edu/eng/main), where I studied multi-omics integration and computational methods for functional genomics with [Jong Wha (Joanne) Joo](https://cblab.yolasite.com/) and [Wonhee Jang](https://sites.google.com/view/dgu-janglab).

I previously worked as a postdoctoral scholar with [Nicholas Mancuso](https://www.mancusolab.com/) and [Serghei Mangul](https://mangul-lab-usc.github.io/) at the University of Southern California, studying genetic associations and integrating genomic data to understand complex disease.
