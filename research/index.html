Research

AI agents, EHR foundation models, spatial and single-cell omics, and integrative genomics.

I develop AI agents and foundation models at the intersection of computational biology and bioinformatics, working across spatial omics, genetics, and clinical data.

01

AI Agents for Biological Discovery

How can biological interpretations from omics analysis be checked against supporting evidence?

Building agent harnesses for context-aware, evidence-grounded biological discovery.

I design agent harnesses that combine task-specific skills, MCP-connected tools and knowledge sources, custom guardrails, and bounded repair loops driven by validation. The workflows adapt to cell and tissue context, with biological interpretations checked against supporting evidence.

  • Task-specific skills
  • MCP
  • Custom guardrails
  • Validation & repair

02

Foundation Models for Electronic Health Records

How can we learn shared clinical representations from notes and structured EHR data?

Building clinical foundation models by aligning clinical notes and structured EHR data through contrastive learning.

Clinical notes provide narrative context, while structured EHR data capture clinical measurements and events. I use contrastive learning to connect these complementary views and learn shared clinical representations.

  • Foundation models
  • Contrastive learning
  • Clinical notes
  • Structured EHR

Spatial & Single-cell Omics

How do cell states and genetic effects vary across tissue regions?

Developing computational tools for spatial omics and single-cell eQTL data.

I am interested in methods that connect molecular measurements to cell states, tissue context, and regulatory variation, building on my experience with functional genomic data.
